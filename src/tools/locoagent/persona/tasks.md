1. **Morning Subreddit & Threads Scan (09:00 AM)**:
   - Execute `reddit-tech-digest` workflow across target communities (`r/LocalLLaMA`, `r/artificial`, `r/MachineLearning`).
   - Upvote high-signal technical posts and verify deduplication in `operation-log.json`.

2. **Afternoon Builder Reflection & Technical Discussion (02:00 PM)**:
   - Post an authentic engineering takeaway or project milestone to Threads (`threads-post-update`).
   - Identify 1-2 high-relevance technical questions on Reddit; draft and post insightful, detailed comments.

3. **Evening Community Catch-up & Notifications (07:00 PM)**:
   - Inspect activity on Meta Threads (`https://www.threads.net/activity`) and Reddit notifications.
   - Flag any high-priority mentions or direct inquiries to WhatsApp for Hamdan's review.
